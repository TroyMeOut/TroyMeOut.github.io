Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, the selected season and week, and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help. Please hide any entry labels or other personal information you do not want to share.

Common questions

How do quarter matches work?

At the end of each completed quarter, the app compares the last digit of each team’s cumulative score with your pair. Either order is the default; choosing “Packers first” requires the first digit to match Green Bay. A score during an unfinished quarter is not a completed-quarter match.

How do final numbers work?

The app adds the digits of each team’s final score separately, then takes the last digit of each sum. For example, 29 gives 2 + 9 = 11 → 1, and 13 gives 1 + 3 = 4. The resulting pair is 1–4. Your chosen pair-order setting also applies to final numbers.

Why didn’t a refresh change the score?

The app reads a shared score service. Manual refresh downloads the latest saved result; it does not force the football data provider to update. Check the “Updated” time in the score section. Provider delays, network problems, and the server’s daily request limit can leave that time unchanged.

When does the app update automatically?

While the app is open, it checks during live games using the interval selected in Settings. Upcoming games wait for kickoff, and completed games stop automatic checks. The shared service also follows its own schedule, so checking more often on your phone does not make the underlying score newer.

Why didn’t I receive an alert?

Enable “Notify me about wins” in the app’s Settings and allow notifications in iPhone Settings. Alerts are generated locally when the app processes an update with a new match. The app does not continuously monitor scores or deliver remote score alerts while closed.

Can I track another week or team?

Use the calendar button to choose a week. Known bye weeks cannot be selected. The current app supports Packers games; other teams are not yet available in the app. Some future or older results may not yet be available from the shared service.

Where are my entries stored?

Your number pairs, labels, and history stay in the app’s local storage on your iPhone. No account is required, and the app has no built-in entry syncing between devices. Your Apple backup settings may affect backup or restoration.

How can I delete my data?

Use the trash icon to remove an entry. To remove all local app storage, use iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Football Numbers → Delete App. Offloading keeps app data; deleting the app removes its local data. Manage any device backups separately in your Apple settings.
