Football Numbers

Privacy policy.

Effective and last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy describes how Football Numbers, developed by Troy Loberger, handles information when you use the iPhone app and its support website.

Your number pairs stay on your iPhone.

The app does not require an account. It does not send your number pairs, entry labels, match history, or notification preferences to the shared score service.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your number pairs and labels, selected weeks, downloaded schedules and scores, calculated matches, notification records, and app preferences in its local storage. This information supports the app’s features and lets you view saved results offline.

The app does not provide its own cloud account or syncing service for your entries. Depending on your Apple settings, device backups or transfers may include app data. Those services are managed by Apple.

Score requests and hosting

The app connects over HTTPS to a shared score service hosted on Cloudflare. Requests include the season, season phase, week, and team identifiers needed to retrieve schedules and scores. Like other internet services, Cloudflare receives connection information such as your IP address, request time, requested address, and available request headers. It may process or retain technical information to deliver and secure the service.

The shared database stores football schedules, score responses, update times, aggregate provider-request counts, and synchronization status. The application code does not create user profiles or store your number entries there. Service diagnostics may record operational information and errors.

The server currently retrieves football data from ESPN for testing. The app requests scores from Cloudflare rather than contacting ESPN’s score API directly. The server does not forward your number pairs, entry labels, or device IP address to ESPN as part of those score requests.

Cloudflare’s handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy. Opening an external link, including an ESPN link, takes you to a service governed by its own policies.

Notifications and device access

Match alerts are optional local notifications. With your permission, the app schedules them on your device when a score update identifies a new match. It does not register a remote push-notification token with the shared score service. You can disable alerts in the app’s Settings or in iPhone notification settings.

The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

Advertising and analytics

The current app includes no advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information or use app information for cross-app advertising tracking.

Support messages

If you email support, Troy Loberger receives your email address and any message, screenshots, or other information you choose to include. That information is used to respond, troubleshoot, and maintain support correspondence. Email is handled through the email provider. Please avoid including sensitive information or number-entry labels you do not wish to share.

Support website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or a contact-form service. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and other technical information for hosting and security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Retention and your choices

Local app information remains until you remove entries or delete the app. To remove its local storage, use iPhone Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Football Numbers → Delete App. Offloading the app preserves its documents and data. Existing device backups are managed separately through your Apple settings.

Shared football records and operational information are retained as needed to run and troubleshoot the service. Hosting-provider retention follows the provider’s policies and service settings. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request and related follow-up; you can request its deletion by email, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

For questions or requests concerning personal information, contact troyloberger@gmail.com. Number entries stored only on your phone cannot be accessed or deleted remotely by the developer.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or profile. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the app’s information practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
